YBase for Teams - Architecture Diagrams

Mermaid diagrams generated from the current FastAPI, React, Postgres, memory, auth, and Slack connector code.

How to read the colors

Blue nodes are services, UI, routes, or normal flow. Green nodes are successful/valid states. Red nodes are errors or denied paths. Amber nodes are warnings, retries, or degraded behavior. Purple nodes are databases, persistence, or planned roadmap work.

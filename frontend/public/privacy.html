YBase Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

YBase ("YBase", "we", "us") is an AI memory layer that connects to the tools your team already uses — Slack, Jira, GitHub, Linear, Confluence, Discord, Google Docs, Notion, and Figma — and turns the decisions and discussions inside them into searchable, cited answers. This policy explains what data we collect through those connections, how we use it, and how you can control or delete it.

1. What we collect

When a workspace admin connects a source, YBase reads only what that connector is scoped for:

  • Account & workspace info — your name, email, and workspace membership, used for login and access control.
  • Connected-tool content — messages, issues, pages, comments, or documents from the specific channels, projects, spaces, or files an admin selects. We do not read code, files, or content outside the streams a workspace explicitly turns on.
  • OAuth tokens — access and refresh tokens for each connected source, stored encrypted at rest and used only to sync that source's content.
  • Usage data — questions asked, features used, and basic request logs, used to operate and improve the product.

2. How we use it

Connected content is processed to build your workspace's memory: extracting decisions, generating embeddings for search, and answering questions with citations back to the original source. This processing uses Anthropic's Claude models and Voyage AI's embedding models as subprocessors — your content is sent to them solely to generate the response or embedding YBase requested, and is not used by us or them to train models on your data.

We do not sell your data. We do not use connected-tool content for advertising.

3. Google Workspace data

If you connect Google Docs, YBase's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We request read-only access (drive.readonly) to Google Docs your account can already see, and use that content only to build the searchable memory described above. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not allow humans to read it except as needed for security, legal compliance, or with your explicit consent for support purposes.

4. Storage & security

Data is stored in an encrypted Postgres database. OAuth tokens are additionally encrypted at the application layer before storage, using a rotating key scheme, so tokens never sit in the database in plaintext even if the database itself were compromised.

5. Data retention & deletion

Disconnecting a source stops future syncing immediately. Deleting a source connection or a workspace removes the associated documents and tokens from our systems, subject to standard backup retention windows. You can request full deletion at any time via the contact below.

6. Your choices

  • Admins choose exactly which channels, projects, spaces, or files are synced — nothing is read by default.
  • Any connected source can be disconnected at any time from the Sources page.
  • You can request an export or deletion of your workspace's data at any time.

7. Changes to this policy

We'll update the date at the top of this page when this policy changes, and note material changes to workspace admins.
